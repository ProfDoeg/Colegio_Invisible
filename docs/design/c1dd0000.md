## The four seed elements

What a reader carries as code, because it needs them to *find and trust*
the estandarte.

### 1. The envelope — six bytes, frozen forever

```
offset  bytes    meaning
0..1    c1 dd    magic — Colegio Invisible / DD
2..3    u16-BE   protocol version   (0x0000 = the constitution)
4       u8       type byte
5       u8       tone byte
```

The magic is two bytes; the version is data, selecting the standard
*before* any body parse. The `0xee` type byte — never a version value —
marks a registry apart from data. `parse_envelope` guards length first
and gates only on the magic (fixes A2 + the short-header IndexError).

### 2. The estandarte body format, inherited across versions

Hardcoded like the envelope: `parent_kind` · `T` type entries · `C`
conventions. **Skeleton only** — grammar-as-data is deliberately excluded
until proven against text `0x00` (c1dd0002 §8); freezing a descriptor
language before that proof would freeze a guessed ceiling into the one
document that cannot be amended. The metacircular `0xee` self-entry names
the type on chain; the byte-layout stays in the repo docstring until the
grammar fold.

### 3. The trust anchor

The **ACH 3-of-3** (Anthony · Christophia · Hayagriva). A reader follows
only the chain ACH certifies; another party's `c1dd..ee` chain is a
different authority's fork, not followed. The concrete anchor waits empty
(`constitution.ACH_ANCHOR`, `root_txid = None`) until inscription.

### 4. The tip rule

From the anchored root: genesis tag → fan-out (despot · amend ·
commentary).

1. **Despot check first** — latent / exercised / burned. Resolving
   before checking the despot walks the reader through the very defect
   it exists to correct.
2. **The constitution's current text** — walk the amend thread
   (constitutional additions, in chain order).
3. **Legislation** — find the v1 registry: the `c1dd0001ee` whose
   `parent_txid` names the constitution root, ACH-signed (the
   constitution's only tag is claimed by genesis, so this one hop is
   text-anchored). From v1, walk succession by thread: each registry
   root's single tag is its cord; the successor's root spends it;
   thread and text agree at every hop; the terminal intact tag is the
   leaf.
4. **Fallback** (dead or absent thread) — scan the version bucket for
   `0xee`, walk `parent_txid` chains, keep only the chain rooted at the
   anchor **and signed at every link** by the then-current authority.
   Authority is transferable; the reader follows the succession.

Commentary is never consulted for law.

## What the constitution contains

A `c1dd0000ee` quipu, **sovereign** tone (`0xee` — the constitutional
register; tone and type share the byte, the estandarte speaking in its
own name):

- the `0xee` **self-entry** (skeleton: no dimensions, no flags);
- the **conventions** — magic, tone, diamond, assembly, tag, genesis,
  despot, amend, commentary, citation, forest, pre-canonical.

Both derive from the single source (`estandarte._example_registry`), so
the constitution cannot drift from the registry it founds. Type
vocabulary is v1 legislation, and growing it does not move the golden;
changing a convention or the self-entry does — deliberate, reviewed
amendment only.

## The genesis fan-out

The constitution root is an **ordinary quipu**: `N+2` outputs — header
strand 0, body strands 1..N, one tag. All machinery lives one hop away.

**The genesis act.** The tag's spend has exactly **three outputs and no
OP_RETURN** — necessarily mute: a spend carrying OP_RETURN *is* strand
continuation and would read as body. Each output is ACH 3-of-3, ordinal:

| # | output | power | burn |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **despot** | override / supersede the constitution | first |
| 2 | **amend** | append to the constitution | second |
| 3 | **commentary** | none — voice only | never |

All three powers are scoped to the **constitution itself**. Legislation
(the version registries, new types) is a separate life with its own
succession (Article V).

**One growth rule.** A thread grows when a quipu's *root* spends its tip
(roots carry no OP_RETURN: mute act at the tip, full quipu one hop
away). The genesis act is the only pure act with no quipu attached, once
ever. Until pulled, the powers are latent and the constitution alone is
the law.

**The burn ladder — three eras of the constitution.** A burn = a spend
offering no successor, value to provably unspendable outputs. Public,
irreversible. The eras are the *constitution's* life — legislation
legislates in all three:

1. **Founding** — despot alive: the constitution is mutable by
   declaration.
2. **Amendment** — despot burned: the constitution grows by addition
   only.
3. **Eternal** — both burned: the constitution is complete; only
   commentary moves. Types are still chain-taught; standards still
   succeed — under law that can no longer change.

**Inception-only by physics.** Outputs cannot be retrofitted; an
amendment procedure adopted by amendment is circular. Now or never —
taken now.

## Article V — amendment and succession

Two separate mechanisms, one discipline (text + instrument, inert until
pulled; the pull *is* the deployment).

**The amend thread — constitutional additions.** Roots of amendment
quipu (`c1dd0000ee`) spend the amend tip, each offering the next.
Additions only, chain order; what the despot may rewrite, the amender
may only extend. Ends at the second burn: the constitution is complete.

**Legislative succession — the ripcord, per registry root.** The
constitution's tag is claimed by genesis, so v1 anchors by text
(`parent_txid` → constitution root, ACH-signed). From v1 onward,
succession is thread: each registry root's single tag is its cord; the
successor's root spends it.

- **Unique by consensus.** At most one spend of a tip confirms — the
  double-spend rule *is* the single-succession rule. Exclusion by
  consensus, not adjudication.
- **An act, not writing** — walked root-to-root, script-free
  (`quipu_tags.follow_ripcord`; survives re-encumbrance).
- **Two pointers, one truth.** Thread forward, `parent_txid` back; the
  ceremony gate refuses disagreement — including a tip spent by an
  OP_RETURN-carrying tx (reads as strand, not act).
- **Degrades, never worse.** Dead thread → the signed `parent_txid`
  scan: exactly the pre-thread rule.
- **Hostile-spend honesty.** Compromised keys were always
  revolution-grade; the thread doesn't create that risk, it timestamps
  it.
- **Never the first performance.** Full rehearsal on a throwaway mainnet
  diamond before the ceremony (`inscriptions_ready/README_estandarte.md`).

## Article VI — the commentary thread

The third output: voice, no power, nothing to renounce.

- **Never statute.** Notices, glosses, warnings — a dead-thread notice,
  a rotation announcement, a forged-chain warning. Resolvers ignore it;
  nothing here alters how a byte parses.
- **Outlives both burns** — in the eternal era it is all that still
  moves.
- **No new vocabulary** — links are identified by position on the
  spend-chain; any type may speak.

## Article VII — the despot

The first output. **First to burn.**

**Purpose.** Healing repairs broken *data*; it cannot repair broken
*law* — healing runs on the law. Against a defect in the seed rules
themselves (a security vulnerability, a logic blackhole, a wedged
succession), the despot is the one instrument outside the rule system it
corrects. The purpose is stated; the power is not.

- **General while alive** — a blackhole is the case not written down;
  an enumerated emergency power protects only against foreseen failures.
  A declaration through the despot binds readers over any ordinary rule.
- **Exercise** = a declaration quipu's root spends the tip and offers a
  fresh one. The declaration names the defect and the correction;
  declarations bind in chain order.
- **Renunciation** = the burn: no successor, value provably
  unspendable. Ends the founding era; the ordinary machinery converts
  from policy-while-trusted to physics-forever.
- **Any successor-less spend ends the power** — physics ignores intent.
  The canonical burn form exists so renunciation is legibly deliberate.
  Hence the **tag quarantine**: intact tags are derived
  (`classify_root_outputs`), subtracted from every funding path,
  spendable only through a declared act's gate.
- **The boundary.** The despot protects against defects in the law, not
  loss of the keys — key compromise stays revolution-grade (Article V).
- **Burn day is policy, not schedule.** Once the machinery is proven
  across real successions, the authority chooses the day.

## The three tiers of change

| change | how | cost |
|---|---|---|
| new **type** | fetch the registry; chain-taught | soft |
| new **estandarte feature** within the descriptor language | amend the registry | soft |
| widen the **descriptor language** itself | new constitution | **hard fork of the seed** |

A reader can learn new vocabulary from the chain; it cannot learn a new
*way of describing vocabulary*, because reading that description is the
capability in question. That asymmetry is what "frozen" means.

While the despot lives, one extraordinary channel sits above this table:
defect repair by declaration. In law it reaches even the hard row — the
despot may ordain a rewrite of the descriptor language, the amender an
addition to it: a constitutional fork, same anchor, same lineage, instead
of a revolution. In physics neither installs capability into deployed
code: the despot check is seed code, so a reader that cannot *apply* a
declaration can still *see* it, and is bound to honest failure — report
"superseded," never misparse. Code still follows law. The first burn
removes the channel; the second removes the soft rows; after that, only
commentary moves.
