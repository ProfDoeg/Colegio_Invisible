Source texts for the consolidated publication (the Book of 108 plan in
`docs/design/book-108-cathedral.md` names ~108 essays; the author
estimates 50–52 in this numbered series). Delivered in batches,
filed VERBATIM — organization lives here, never edits to the text.
Candidate errata are LOGGED below for the author's own pass; the
files are untouched.

Numbering runs DESCENDING from 220. Each file: number+title line,
English text, separator, Spanish text — exactly as received.
Exception: the texts for 200–191 and 190–181 arrived WITHOUT number
prefixes on their title lines; the `NNN TITLE` header line in those
files was added from the author's own batch lists (organization, not
an edit). 184 arrived with no title line at all — its full header
comes from the batch list.

## Connective threads (for the eventual book structure)

- The Vates 'Domrémy' embroidery is the corpus's anchor object (212),
  read outward through fates (211), martyrs (213), thread-craft (214),
  gender (215), sword (216), standard (217), atavism (218),
  trauma/trama (219), coronation (220).
- The ombú tree binds Lorraine to La Pampa (212, 217, 218).
- 217 EL ESTANDARTE speaks directly to the protocol's own Estandarte
  (0xee) — standards, cryptography (SHA-256, secp256k1), state, field.
- 219 names the 108 suitors of Penelope; 212 carries a 32-byte hex
  payload (`6da7a9a9…4910`) "encoded by the psychopomps of the Winter
  Triangle" — connects to the cemetery sky. Corpus object of interest;
  no action taken.
- The narrator is El Ermitaño-adjacent: first-person passages reference
  the Ivory Palace expulsion (219), the grandmother of the Lower East
  Side (213), walks in Cazón with Tomás (217).
- The narrator is explicitly CYNOCEPHALUS — "My canine head puts me in
  good company" (206); the dog-headed line runs Hayagriva (210) →
  Anubis (206) → Saint Christopher (208), and 180 names the hermit
  directly (erēmitēs — El Ermitaño's etymology).
- The corpus names the project itself: 205 has "Yuki Seo, the profesor
  of knots and fabrics of the Invisible College"; 202 stages the
  narrator's induction and asks whether the College's occultness is
  "for our protection or with the intention to disappear us."
- Goethe / Italian Journey cluster (194, 191, 189, 186, 182) speaks
  directly to the Gana forest already on chain (block 6,237,154);
  193 GANAS + 196 NIGHT WRITING carry the ganas/voluntad axis of the
  inscribed Keyserling essays. 191 exits the Journey into Monte
  Verità / Laban / OTO — the dance thread.
- Subterranean / hollow-earth cycle: 204 (Symmes, hornero oven),
  181, 179, 178, 175 — Phrygian caps recur (204 hornero, 205 Santa
  & elves, 206 the Saqsaywaman guide).
- Money/extraction thread: Potosí mit'a & the $-sign (204), commodity
  fetishism (205), Capacocha (201), 1492 expulsion & Columbus (197),
  court Jews (187) — capital as dead labor, provenance as erasure.
- Jewish history & legibility cycle: 197, 192, 190, 187, 185, 183,
  181, 179, 173 — clothing decrees, hats, binaries of legibility.
- Andean fieldwork arc: the narrator travels Lima → Cusco (205, 206,
  201) following the ceques; ties batch 1's Cusco material (220) to
  the middle of the corpus.
