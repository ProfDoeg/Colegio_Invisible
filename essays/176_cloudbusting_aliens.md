176 CLOUDBUSTING ALIENS

The song ‘Cloudbusting’ was released as a single from the 1985 album ‘Hounds of Love’. The accompanying music video was jointly conceived by Kate Bush and Terry Gilliam and reads as an exercise in fantastical realism. It depicts episodes in 1957 as recounted by Peter Reich in his 1973 ‘A Book of Dreams’. Donald Sutherland plays Wilhelm Reich, during cognitive decline and paranoid episodes, right before his US federal imprisonment and subsequent death. Kate Bush plays the 13-year-old son Peter, operating the cloudbuster.

Wilhelm Reich was greatly affected by the body psyche magic power being cultivated in Vienna and Berlin in the 1920s and 1930s. Like Maria Orsic, Reich was influenced by Edward Bulwer Lytton’s ‘The Power of the Coming Race’ and similar period narratives. Reich was apprehensive about anti-Semitic narratives, and by the early 1930s, he was sure that the analysis of Freud and Marx were conceptual catalysts for birthing the vanguard race.

In his last book, ‘Contact With Space: Oranur’ (1956), Wilhelm Reich details an ongoing galactic war between the Army of Cosmic Engineers and fascist pro-nuclear aliens who plague the atmosphere in spacecraft that consume beneficial Orgone Energy and emit Deadly Orgone Radiation as exhaust. Wilhelm Reich details his interactions with the Extraterrestrial Aliens, his numerous reports to US military intelligence, and his ongoing resistance to the threat using his cloudbuster technology. In the text, Reich describes seeing the 1951 movie ‘The Day the Earth Stood Still’ and afterward feeling a state of unreality. He found himself born into this moment as an alienated vanguard, as cynocephalus.

Wilhelm Reich fue profundamente afectado por el poder mágico del cuerpo y la psique que se cultivaba en Viena y Berlín en los años 1920 y 1930 temprano. Al igual que Maria Orsic, Reich fue influenciado por 'El Poder de la Raza Venidera' de Edward Bulwer Lytton y narrativas similares de la época. Reich estaba preocupado por las narrativas antisemitas, y a principios de los años 1930, estaba seguro de que el análisis de Freud y Marx junto eran catalizadores conceptuales para dar a luz a la raza vanguardista.

En su último libro, 'Contacto con el Espacio: Oranur' (1956), Wilhelm Reich detalla una guerra galáctica en curso entre el Ejército de Ingenieros Cósmicos y los alienígenas fascistas pronucleares que plagaban la atmósfera en naves espaciales que consumen Energía Orgone beneficiosa y emiten Radiación Orgone Mortal como escape. Wilhelm Reich detalla sus interacciones con los Alienígenas Extraterrestres, sus numerosos informes a la inteligencia militar de los EE. UU., y su resistencia continua a la amenaza utilizando su tecnología rompenubes. En el texto, Reich describe haber visto la película de 1951 'El Día que la Tierra se Detuvo' y después sentir un estado de irrealidad. Se encontró nacido en este momento como una vanguardia alienada, como cinocéfalo.

¿Soy un hombre del espacio? ¿Pertenezco a una nueva raza en la tierra, criada por hombres del espacio exterior...?
