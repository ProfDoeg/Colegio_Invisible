187 COURT JEWS

Pharaoh took his signet ring from his finger and put it on Joseph’s finger. He dressed him in robes of fine linen and put a gold chain around his neck. He had him ride in his second-commander chariot, and people shouted before him, “Make way and bow down.” Thus Pharaoh put Joseph in charge of the whole land of Egypt.

Genesis 41:41-43

Being in the lord’s court as an outsider is precarious because executive power is irrational and characterized by wild swings of uncertainty, treachery, and violence. While proximity to the sovereign is dangerous, one may gain influence and the royal gold chain. Joseph, sold into slavery by his brothers, and imprisoned after false accusations of sexual impropriety, rose to greatness through his psychoanalytic divination and effective state administration of resources. Informed by Pharaoh’s dream of fat and lean calves, Joseph ordered that 20% of agricultural production be saved during seven years of bounty. Egypt accumulated great wealth and fed the world when the grain stores were opened during the seven years of famine.

Daniel too would serve as an interpreter of dreams, visions, and signs for Nebuchadnezzar, Belshazzar, Darius, and Cyrus during the Babylonian Exile. During the Feast of Belshazzar a mystical occurrence took place. With supernatural writing on the wall, Daniel became the deft reader and interpreter.

Fingers of a disembodied human hand began writing on the wall. The king watched the hand. His face turned pale, and his thoughts terrified him. His limbs collapsed and his knees knocked together. The king called in the enchanters, Chaldeans, and diviners; saying to the wise men of Babylon “Whoever can read this writing shall be clothed in purple, have a gold chain on his neck, and ranked number three.”

Belshazzar gave the command and Daniel was clothed in purple, a chain of gold was put around his neck, and Daniel was proclaimed to rank third in the kingdom. That very night Belshazzar, the Chaldean king, was killed, and Darius the Mede received the kingdom.

Daniel 5:5-7 25-30

While Daniel received the reward for his interpretation of the writing on the wall, his rise to prominence would land him in the den of lions after the overthrow of the monarch. Even if you commune with Gabriel, proximity to power is fundamentally perilous. You may draw ire, fall victim when your patron is overthrown.

Aaron of Lincoln (1125-1186) was the wealthiest man of Norman England. He financed aristocrats and the crown of England, including the great stonework abbeys and monasteries, such as St Alban’s Cathedral, Lincoln Cathedral, and Peterborough Cathedral. On Aaron’s death, King Henry II established a special Exchequer for Aaron’s estate, seized its assets, and dissolved a majority of the royal debts due to the estate. This organization became the Exchequer of the Jews, which handled all financial dealings with the tribe until Edward I expelled them from all of England in 1290. The Edict of Expulsion stayed in effect until 1656.


Vivelin of Strasbourg, associate of the influential Baldwin of Luxembourg, lent the King of England, Edward III, 140,000 florins in 1339 at the start of the 100 Years War that brought the extended English occupation of France. A decade later, Vivelin met a bitter end during the Strasbourg Valentine’s Day pogrom of 1349, in which over 2000 were burned at the stake. The tribe of Strasbourg was subsequently banished from the city and economically quarantined within the cloth and cattle trades. Each night at 22 hours, the Grüselhorn would sound to prompt the tribe to leave the city.

The 14th century saw an extended process of expulsion of the tribe from the whole of France, beginning with King Philip IV's order of 1306 and ending with Charles VI's 1394 Valentine’s Day decree. The 1322 burning alive and expulsion of the tribe from Metz was another punctuated moment. As the Black Plague swept across Europe, another crescendo of violence emerged in the Rhineland when expulsions and murders took place in Mainz, Basel, and Strasbourg, with accusations that the tribe was intentionally spreading the plague. It was not until the 1791 National Assembly of the French Revolution that the tribe was granted French citizenship. Napoleon’s 1806 Assembly of Jewish Notables and 1807 Grand Sanhedrin helped cement the tribe's status in France after centuries of uncertainty.

Twenty years after the end of the 30 Years War, Vienna expelled the tribe, destroying homes and schule, accusing the tribe of extractive economic practices and collusion with the Ottoman Empire. Soon after the expulsion, Samuel Oppenheimer (1630-1703) arrived in Vienna to serve the Habsburg Emperor of the Holy Roman Empire, Leopold I, as a military supplier and oberfaktor. After his death, Samuel’s estate underwent a similar conversion to that of Aaron of Lincoln. All debts owed by the state to Samuel were dissolved, and his family lost great wealth.

Samuel’s nephew, Joseph Süss Oppenheimer (1698-1738), served in a similar capacity to his uncle for Duke Karl Alexander in the Duchy of Württemberg in Stuttgart. Joseph, known as “Jud Süss”, accumulated much power, wealth, and influence while in Alexander’s court. He lived outside the Judengasse, in the Golden Swan Inn, and socialized with women outside the tribe. When Karl Alexander died in 1737, Joseph was brought up on charges of fraud, corruption, and lechery. He was tried and sentenced to death by hanging. After the execution, his body was placed outside the city in an elevated cage and left to decompose for six years.

The details of the case were kept secret by the state until 1918. In 1925, Lion Feuchtwanger, a tribesman of Weimar Germany, published the novel “Jud Süss”, with a textured and sympathetic interpretation of the protagonist. In 1934, the German-born director Lothar Mendes created a film adaptation of Feuchtwanger’s novel, titled “Jew Süss”. In a direct and symbolic response, Joseph Goebbels contracted German director Veit Harlan to create an opposing film, titled “Jud Süss”. The 1940 work presented Joseph as a villain. Heinrich Himmler required the Schutzstaffel and police to view the film.

Aarón de Lincoln (1125-1186) fue el hombre más rico de la Inglaterra normanda. Financió a aristócratas y la corona de Inglaterra, incluyendo las grandes abadías y monasterios de piedra, como la Catedral de St Alban, la Catedral de Lincoln y la Catedral de Peterborough. A la muerte de Aarón, el rey Enrique II estableció un Exchequer especial para la propiedad de Aarón, se apoderó de sus activos y disolvió la mayoría de las deudas reales debido a la propiedad. Esta organización se convirtió en el Exchequer de los Judíos, que manejaba todas las transacciones financieras con la tribu hasta que Eduardo I los expulsó de toda Inglaterra en 1290. El Edicto de Expulsión permaneció en efecto hasta 1656.

Vivelin de Estrasburgo, asociado del influyente Baldwin de Luxemburgo, prestó al Rey de Inglaterra, Eduardo III, 140,000 florines en 1339 al comienzo de la Guerra de los 100 Años que trajo la prolongada ocupación inglesa de Francia. Una década después, Vivelin encontró un amargo final durante el pogromo del Día de San Valentín de 1349 en Estrasburgo, en el que más de 2000 fueron quemados en la hoguera. La tribu de Estrasburgo fue posteriormente desterrada de la ciudad y económicamente cuarentenada dentro de los comercios de tela y ganado. Cada noche a las 22 horas, el Grüselhorn sonaba para indicar a la tribu que abandonara la ciudad. 

 El siglo XIV vio un extenso proceso de expulsión de la tribu de toda Francia, comenzando con la orden de Felipe IV en 1306 y terminando con el decreto del Día de San Valentín de 1394 de Carlos VI. La quema viva y expulsión de la tribu de Metz en 1322 fue otro momento puntual. Mientras la Peste Negra se extendía por Europa, otro crescendo de violencia surgió en el Rin cuando se llevaron a cabo expulsiones y asesinatos en Maguncia, Basilea y Estrasburgo, con acusaciones de que la tribu estaba esparciendo intencionalmente la peste. No fue hasta la Asamblea Nacional de 1791 de la Revolución Francesa que la tribu obtuvo la ciudadanía francesa. La Asamblea de Notables Judíos de 1806 de Napoleón y el Gran Sanedrín de 1807 ayudaron a cimentar el estatus de la tribu en Francia después de siglos de incertidumbre. 

Veinte años después del fin de la Guerra de los 30 Años, Viena expulsó a la tribu, destruyendo hogares y schule, acusándolos de prácticas económicas extractivas y colusión con el Imperio Otomano. Poco después de la expulsión, Samuel Oppenheimer (1630-1703) llegó a Viena para servir al Emperador de los Habsburgo del Sacro Imperio Romano Germánico, Leopoldo I, como proveedor militar y oberfaktor. Tras su muerte, la propiedad de Samuel sufrió una conversión similar a la de Aarón de Lincoln. Todas las deudas que el estado tenía con Samuel fueron disueltas, y su familia perdió una gran riqueza.

El sobrino de Samuel, Joseph Süss Oppenheimer (1698-1738), sirvió en una capacidad similar a la de su tío para el Duque Karl Alexander en el Ducado de Württemberg en Stuttgart. Joseph, conocido como "Jud Süss", acumuló mucho poder, riqueza e influencia mientras estuvo en la corte de Alexander. Vivió fuera de la Judengasse, en la posada Cisne Dorado, y socializaba con mujeres fuera de la tribu. Cuando Karl Alexander murió en 1737, Joseph fue acusado de fraude, corrupción y lujuria. Fue juzgado y condenado a muerte por ahorcamiento. Después de la ejecución, su cuerpo fue colocado fuera de la ciudad en una jaula elevada y dejado para descomponerse durante seis años.

Los detalles del caso fueron mantenidos en secreto por el estado hasta 1918. En 1925, Lion Feuchtwanger, miembro de la tribu de la Alemania de Weimar, publicó la novela "Jud Süss", con una interpretación texturizada y simpática del protagonista. En 1934, el director alemán Lothar Mendes creó una adaptación cinematográfica de la novela de Feuchtwanger, titulada "Jew Süss". En una respuesta directa y simbólica, Joseph Goebbels contrató al director alemán Veit Harlan para crear una película opuesta, titulada "Jud Süss". La obra de 1940 presentaba a Joseph como un villano. Heinrich Himmler exigió que las Schutzstaffel y la policía vieran la película.
