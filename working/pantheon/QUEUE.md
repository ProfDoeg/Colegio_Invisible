essay + Instagram corpus, approved 2026-09-06 (all borderline cases
included per the author's direction). Pipeline: GOD_DOSSIER_PROMPT.md

step. One exception noted below: the twelve Dvergatal/Tolkien dwarf names
are bundled as a single dossier rather than split twelve ways, since in
the corpus they appear only as a shared list/company, not as individually
differentiated myths -- flagged for the author to override if he wants
