FIGURE. Naphtali: second son of Jacob and Bilhah. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'my wrestlings' (Gen 30:8); 'a hind let loose: he giveth goodly words' (Gen 49:21) with the LXX 'spreading terebinth' variant; 'satisfied with favour… possess thou the west and the south' (Deut 33:23); the Galilee allotment (Josh 19:32-39), Hazor, Kedesh, Chinnereth, the Sea of Galilee; Barak of Kedesh and Deborah's war (Judg 4-5); Hiram's mother of Naphtali (1 Kgs 7:14); the first deportation under Tiglath-Pileser (2 Kgs 15:29); Isaiah 9:1 and Matthew 4:13-16 'land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali… Galilee of the nations' and Jesus at Capernaum; Tobit of Naphtali; the stone: amethyst/ahlamah; the banner: wine-red with a hind; Blavatsky: Naphtali as Capricorn? (the hind/goat) verbatim; the Testament of Naphtali.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
