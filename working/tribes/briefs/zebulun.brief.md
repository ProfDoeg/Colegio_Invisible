FIGURE. Zebulun: sixth son of Jacob and Leah. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'dwelling/honour' (Gen 30:20); 'Zebulun shall dwell at the haven of the sea; and he shall be for an haven of ships; and his border shall be unto Zidon' (Gen 49:13); 'rejoice, Zebulun, in thy going out' (Deut 33:18); the lower Galilee allotment (Josh 19:10-16), Nazareth's region, Bethlehem of Galilee, Gath-hepher (Jonah of Zebulun); Elon the judge (Judg 12:11-12); Deborah's praise 'jeoparded their lives unto the death' (Judg 5:18); Isa 9:1 and Matthew 4; the stone: diamond/yahalom; the banner: white with a ship; Blavatsky: Zebulun as Pisces? or Cancer? verbatim; the merchant tribe and the Issachar partnership.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
