FIGURE. Reuben: first son of Jacob and Leah. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'unstable as water, thou shalt not excel' (Gen 49:3-4), the mandrakes (Gen 30:14), Bilhah (Gen 35:22), the pit and Joseph (Gen 37:21-22, 29); Deut 33:6 'let Reuben live and not die'; the Transjordan allotment with Gad (Num 32, Josh 13:15-23), Heshbon, Dibon, Aroer, the Mesha stele's Gadites; the Song of Deborah's reproach (Judg 5:15-16); the altar of witness (Josh 22); the stone: sardius/ruby (odem); the banner: red with mandrakes (Bamidbar Rabbah 2:7); Blavatsky: Reuben as Aquarius, the water-bearer, 'unstable as water' (check Isis Unveiled II and SD I for the exact page); the lost-tribe fate east of the Jordan under Tiglath-Pileser III (1 Chr 5:26).

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
