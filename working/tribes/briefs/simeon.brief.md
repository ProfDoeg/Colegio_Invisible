FIGURE. Simeon: second son of Jacob and Leah. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'hearing' (Gen 29:33); Shechem and Dinah with Levi (Gen 34); the hostage in Egypt (Gen 42:24); Jacob's curse 'instruments of cruelty… I will divide them in Jacob' (Gen 49:5-7); absent from Moses' blessing (Deut 33) and the reasons given; the allotment inside Judah (Josh 19:1-9), Beersheba, Ziklag, Hormah; Zimri and the Baal-Peor affair (Num 25); the diminished census; the Simeonites' migration to Gedor and Mount Seir (1 Chr 4:24-43); the stone: topaz/pitdah; the banner: green with Shechem; Blavatsky and the Simeon-Levi pair as Gemini (or Pisces in other schemes): quote her; the Testament of Simeon on envy.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
