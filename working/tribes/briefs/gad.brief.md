FIGURE. Gad: first son of Jacob and Zilpah, Leah's maid. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'a troop cometh' / 'good fortune' (Gen 30:11) and the deity Gad (Isa 65:11); 'a troop shall overcome him, but he shall overcome at the last' (Gen 49:19); 'he dwelleth as a lion… he executed the justice of the LORD' (Deut 33:20-21); the Transjordan allotment with Reuben (Num 32), Gilead, Jazer, Ramoth-gilead; the Mesha stele: 'the men of Gad had dwelt in Ataroth from of old'; Jephthah the Gileadite?; Elijah the Tishbite of Gilead; the Gadites who came to David 'faces like lions' (1 Chr 12:8); the stone: agate/shevo; the banner: grey/black-white with a camp; Blavatsky: Gad as Aries? verbatim; deported 732 BCE.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
