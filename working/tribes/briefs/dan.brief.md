FIGURE. Dan: first son of Jacob and Bilhah, Rachel's maid. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'judge' (Gen 30:6); 'Dan shall be a serpent by the way, an adder in the path… I have waited for thy salvation, O LORD' (Gen 49:16-18); 'a lion's whelp: he shall leap from Bashan' (Deut 33:22); the coastal allotment lost to the Amorites and the migration north to Laish/Dan (Judg 18) with Micah's idol and the Levite; Samson (Judg 13-16); Oholiab the craftsman (Exod 31:6); Jeroboam's golden calf at Dan (1 Kgs 12:29); 'from Dan to Beersheba'; the Tel Dan stele and the 'House of David'; Dan omitted from Revelation 7 and the Antichrist-from-Dan tradition (Irenaeus, Hippolytus); the stone: leshem (jacinth/ligure); the banner: sapphire-blue with a serpent (or scales); Blavatsky: Dan as Scorpio, the serpent, and the eagle, verbatim; the Beta Israel of Ethiopia as Dan (Eldad ha-Dani, the 1973 Ovadia Yosef ruling); the Danites and the Danaans/Tuatha Dé Danann claim (British Israelism).

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
