# Issachar: research brief

FIGURE. Issachar: fifth son of Jacob and Leah. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'hire/reward' (Gen 30:18) and the mandrakes bargain; 'a strong ass couching down between two burdens… became a servant unto tribute' (Gen 49:14-15); 'rejoice, Zebulun, in thy going out, and Issachar, in thy tents' (Deut 33:18-19); the Jezreel valley allotment (Josh 19:17-23), Jezreel, Shunem, Mount Tabor, Endor; Tola the judge (Judg 10:1); Baasha king of Israel of Issachar; 'the children of Issachar, which were men that had understanding of the times' (1 Chr 12:32) and the tradition that Issachar produced the astronomers and calendar-makers of the Sanhedrin; the stone: sapphire/sappir (lapis lazuli); the banner: black with sun and moon; Blavatsky: Issachar as Taurus? (the ass/ox) verbatim; the Zebulun-Issachar partnership of merchant and scholar (Bereshit Rabbah 99:9).

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
