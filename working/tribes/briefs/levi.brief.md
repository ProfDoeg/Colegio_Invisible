FIGURE. Levi: third son of Jacob and Leah. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'joined' (Gen 29:34); Shechem (Gen 34); the curse of Gen 49:5-7 turned into priesthood (Deut 33:8-11, Thummim and Urim); the golden calf and the Levites' sword (Exod 32:26-29); Moses, Aaron, Miriam, Korah, Phinehas; no territorial allotment, the 48 Levitical cities and the six cities of refuge (Num 35, Josh 21); the Kohathites, Gershonites, Merarites and the tabernacle; the priesthood and the Levites in the Temple, the Zadokites, the Hasmoneans; the stone: emerald/bareqet; the banner: a third white, a third black, a third red, with the Urim and Thummim; Blavatsky's assignment (Gemini with Simeon, or Libra?) verbatim; the Levites today (Cohen and Levi surnames, the Cohen Modal Haplotype genetics), the Samaritan priesthood.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
