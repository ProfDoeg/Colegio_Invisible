Twelve tribes of Israel research, Anthony 2026-09-14, separate from the atlas. Appended to

FIGURE. Judah: fourth son of Jacob and Leah. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'praise' (Gen 29:35); Tamar (Gen 38); the surety for Benjamin (Gen 43-44); 'Judah is a lion's whelp… the sceptre shall not depart from Judah… until Shiloh come' (Gen 49:8-12) with the Shiloh crux; Deut 33:7; the largest allotment (Josh 15), Hebron, Bethlehem, Jerusalem's border; Caleb, Othniel, Achan, Boaz, Jesse, David and the whole Davidic line, the kingdom of Judah 931-586, Hezekiah, Josiah, the exile and return, 'Jew' from Judah; the lion of Judah (Ethiopia's emperors, Revelation 5:5, the Rastafari); the stone: carbuncle/nofekh (or emerald); the banner: sky-blue with a lion; Blavatsky: Judah as Leo, verbatim; the Messiah ben David; Judah in Islamic tradition.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
