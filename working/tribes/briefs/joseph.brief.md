FIGURE. Joseph: eleventh son of Jacob, first of Rachel; with Ephraim and Manasseh. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'may he add' (Gen 30:24); the whole Joseph novella (Gen 37-50): the coat, the dreams, the pit, Potiphar, the prison, Pharaoh's dreams, Zaphenath-paneah, Asenath, the famine, the reconciliation, the bones carried to Shechem; 'Joseph is a fruitful bough… the archers have sorely grieved him… his bow abode in strength' (Gen 49:22-26); 'his glory is like the firstling of his bullock, and his horns are like the horns of unicorns' (Deut 33:13-17); Ephraim and Manasseh adopted by Jacob and the crossed hands (Gen 48); the two half-tribes, the hill country of Ephraim and both banks for Manasseh (Josh 16-17), Shechem, Shiloh, Bethel, Samaria; Joshua of Ephraim, Gideon and Jephthah? of Manasseh, Jeroboam of Ephraim and the northern kingdom as 'Ephraim' in Hosea; the Samaritans as Joseph's remnant to this day; the stones: onyx/shoham (Joseph) and the Ephraim/Manasseh assignments; the banner: black with Egypt (Joseph), Ephraim's bullock and Manasseh's wild ox; Blavatsky: Joseph as Sagittarius (the archer), verbatim, and where she puts Ephraim and Manasseh; Messiah ben Joseph; British Israelism's Ephraim = Britain, Manasseh = USA; the Bnei Menashe of Manipur and Mizoram; Joseph in the Qur'an (Sura 12).

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
