FIGURE. Benjamin: twelfth son of Jacob, second of Rachel. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'son of my right hand' / Ben-oni 'son of my sorrow' (Gen 35:18), Rachel's death at Ephrath; the silver cup (Gen 44); 'Benjamin shall ravin as a wolf: in the morning he shall devour the prey, and at night he shall divide the spoil' (Gen 49:27); 'the beloved of the LORD shall dwell in safety by him… he shall dwell between his shoulders' (Deut 33:12) and the Temple on Benjamin's land; the allotment between Judah and Ephraim (Josh 18:11-28), Jerusalem, Jericho, Gibeah, Gibeon, Bethel, Mizpah, Ramah; the outrage at Gibeah and the near-extinction of the tribe (Judg 19-21); Ehud the left-handed judge (Judg 3), the 700 left-handed slingers (Judg 20:16); Saul of Gibeah, the first king; Mordecai and Esther 'a Benjamite'; Paul of Tarsus 'of the tribe of Benjamin' (Rom 11:1, Phil 3:5); the stone: jasper/yashfeh; the banner: many-coloured with a wolf; Blavatsky: Benjamin as ? verbatim; Benjamin with Judah as the surviving southern kingdom.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
