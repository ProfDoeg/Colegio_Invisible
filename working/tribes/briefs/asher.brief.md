FIGURE. Asher: second son of Jacob and Zilpah. Give at the top the name, its meaning, the mother, the birth order,
the emblem, the breastplate stone, the banner, the month, the camp direction, and the zodiac sign
Blavatsky assigns.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: 'happy' (Gen 30:13); 'out of Asher his bread shall be fat, and he shall yield royal dainties' (Gen 49:20); 'let Asher be blessed with children… let him dip his foot in oil… thy shoes shall be iron and brass' (Deut 33:24-25); the coastal allotment north of Carmel (Josh 19:24-31), Acco, Tyre and Sidon on its border, the olive oil of the Galilee; Deborah's reproach 'Asher continued on the sea shore' (Judg 5:17); Anna the prophetess of Asher (Luke 2:36); the stone: beryl/tarshish (or chrysolite); the banner: pearl-coloured with an olive tree; Blavatsky: Asher as Libra? verbatim; the daughters of Asher in Midrash (Serah bat Asher who never died).

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
rabbinic tradition, esoteric correspondence, modern claim, legend. Where a thread above carries a
question mark, treat it as a lead to check and report what you found; for Blavatsky, quote the
passage with volume and page rather than paraphrasing, and if she gives no explicit sign for this
tribe, say so and give the nearest thing she does say. Absence of evidence is a finding.
