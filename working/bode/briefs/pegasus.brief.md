FIGURE. Pegasus: one of Ptolemy's 48 (Almagest, c. 150 CE), kept by the IAU in 1922. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the winged horse born from Medusa's blood, tamer of Bellerophon, striker of the Hippocrene spring, bearer of Zeus's thunderbolt (Hesiod Theog., Eratosthenes 18, Hyginus 2.18, Ovid Met. 4-5); Ptolemy's Hippos drawn without wings and only the front half; the Great Square with Alpheratz taken by Andromeda; Enif, Scheat, Markab; 51 Pegasi b, the first exoplanet around a sun-like star (1995); M15; Schiller's Archangel Gabriel; the Babylonian Field IKU; the Chinese Encampment and Wall; the Arabic 'the horse' and the Bedouin bucket.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
