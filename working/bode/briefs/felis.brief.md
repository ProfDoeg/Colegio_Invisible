FIGURE. Felis: invented by Lalande in 1799 (Bode's Uranographia 1801) from stars of Hydra's back and Antlia, abolished by the IAU in 1922. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the cat, Lalande's joke: 'I am very fond of cats; I will let this figure scratch on the chart' (his own words in the Bibliographie astronomique); the stars near Hydra and Antlia; its recovery in 2018 when the IAU's working group named HD 85951 'Felis'; the cat in the Egyptian sky as a different thing; Jamieson's plate of 1822 with the cat.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
