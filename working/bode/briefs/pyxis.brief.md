# Pyxis: research brief

Bode collection research, Anthony 2026-09-12, separate from the atlas. Appended to

FIGURE. Pyxis: introduced by Lacaille from the Cape of Good Hope (1751-52, published 1756), kept by the IAU. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: Pyxis Nautica, the mariner's compass, Lacaille's 'la Boussole' placed on Argo's mast, the only anachronism aboard the Argonauts' ship; Bode's Lochium Funis (the log line, abolished, separate dossier) beside it; T Pyxidis the recurrent nova; the Pyxis globular; the disputed membership in Argo.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
