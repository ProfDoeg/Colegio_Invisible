FIGURE. Puppis: introduced by Lacaille from the Cape of Good Hope (1751-52, published 1756), kept by the IAU. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the stern (poop deck) of Argo Navis, the largest piece of Lacaille's division of the ship; the Argonaut myth shared with Carina and Vela (assign each dossier its part: here the stern with the speaking beam of Dodona, the Argo's return, and the ship's dedication at Corinth); Naos, Zeta Puppis the hottest naked-eye star; M46, M47; the Gum Nebula; the Puppis A supernova remnant; the Vela-Puppis stream; the Bayer letters without alpha, beta, gamma.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
