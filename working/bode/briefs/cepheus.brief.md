FIGURE. Cepheus: one of Ptolemy's 48 (Almagest, c. 150 CE), kept by the IAU in 1922. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the king of Ethiopia, husband of Cassiopeia, father of Andromeda, son of Belus (Hyginus 2.9, Apollodorus 2.4); the Ethiopia question (which Ethiopia, Joppa or the Nile); Delta Cephei the prototype Cepheid variable (Goodricke 1784) and Leavitt's law; Mu Cephei Herschel's Garnet Star; Alderamin the right arm; the pole star to come (Alrai, Alderamin); Schiller's St Stephen; the Chinese Zaofu the charioteer; the Bedouin shepherd and sheep.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
