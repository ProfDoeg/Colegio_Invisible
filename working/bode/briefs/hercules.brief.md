FIGURE. Hercules: one of Ptolemy's 48 (Almagest, c. 150 CE), kept by the IAU in 1922. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the Kneeler, Engonasin, whom Aratus would not name, identified as Heracles by Eratosthenes (4) and Hyginus (2.6): kneeling on Draco's head at the garden of the Hesperides, or Theseus lifting the stone, or Orpheus, or Thamyris; the Twelve Labors as a sky map; Rasalgethi the kneeler's head; the Keystone and M13 with the 1974 Arecibo message; the Hercules cluster of galaxies; the solar apex, where the Sun is heading; Schiller's Three Magi; the Chinese Heavenly Market; Bode's plate with the club and the lion skin; Cerberus in his hand (Bode's abolished constellation, separate dossier).

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
