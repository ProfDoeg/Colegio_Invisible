FIGURE. Draco: one of Ptolemy's 48 (Almagest, c. 150 CE), kept by the IAU in 1922. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: Ladon the dragon of the Hesperides killed by Heracles (Eratosthenes 3, Hyginus 2.3, Apollodorus 2.5), or the dragon Athena threw into the sky in the war of the giants, or the Python; Thuban the pole star of the pyramid builders (c. 2800 BCE) and the Great Pyramid's shafts; Eltanin and Bradley's discovery of aberration (1725-28); the Cat's Eye Nebula; the Babylonian dragon of Marduk; the Chinese Purple Enclosure's dragon; Schiller's Holy Innocents; the Draconids; the coils between the Bears in Aratus.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
