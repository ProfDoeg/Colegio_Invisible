FIGURE. Piscis Austrinus: one of Ptolemy's 48 (Almagest, c. 150 CE), kept by the IAU in 1922. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the Southern Fish drinking the water of Aquarius, the parent of the zodiac's fishes, the fish that saved Isis or Derceto at Bambyce (Hyginus 2.41, Eratosthenes 38); Fomalhaut the fish's mouth, one of the four royal stars of Persian tradition, and its debris ring and the disputed planet Fomalhaut b (2008-2020); the 'lonely star of autumn'; Schiller's St Matthew's barrel? (verify: Schiller made it the Barrel of Meal of the widow of Zarephath); the Chinese Northern Dipper? (no); the Arabic al-Hut al-Janubi.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
