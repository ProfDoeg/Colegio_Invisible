FIGURE. Vela: introduced by Lacaille from the Cape of Good Hope (1751-52, published 1756), kept by the IAU. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the sails of Argo Navis; the Argonaut myth's part for the sails (the Argo under sail from Iolcus to Colchis, the voyage's stages, the return by the Danube or the Rhône); the Vela pulsar and supernova remnant (the Gum Nebula's source), the first optical pulsar? (no: the Crab) the second; Gamma Velorum Regor (the Apollo astronauts' joke names Regor, Navi, Dnoces); the False Cross; the Eight-Burst Nebula; Suhail al-Muhlif.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
