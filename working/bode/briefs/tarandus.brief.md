FIGURE. Tarandus vel Rangifer: invented by Lemonnier in 1736 as Le Renne, the reindeer, for Maupertuis's Lapland expedition, on Bode's Uranographia 1801, abolished by the IAU in 1922. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the reindeer between Cassiopeia and Camelopardalis, for the 1736-37 expedition to Tornio that measured the arc of meridian and proved the earth flattened at the poles (Maupertuis, Clairaut, Celsius, Lemonnier); the Sami reindeer sky as the real one in the same region; Custos Messium beside it; the stars back in Cassiopeia and Camelopardalis.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
