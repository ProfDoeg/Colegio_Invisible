FIGURE. Auriga: one of Ptolemy's 48 (Almagest, c. 150 CE), kept by the IAU in 1922. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the charioteer: Erichthonius the lame inventor of the four-horse chariot (Eratosthenes 13, Hyginus 2.13), Myrtilus, Phaethon, Hippolytus; Capella the she-goat Amalthea and the Kids on the charioteer's arm, the goat nursing Zeus; the star Elnath shared with Taurus until 1930; the Babylonian Crook GAM; the Kids as a weather sign in Aratus, Theocritus, Horace; Schiller's St Jerome; epsilon Aurigae's eclipses; the Flaming Star Nebula.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
