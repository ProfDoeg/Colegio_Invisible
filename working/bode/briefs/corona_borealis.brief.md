FIGURE. Corona Borealis: one of Ptolemy's 48 (Almagest, c. 150 CE), kept by the IAU in 1922. On Johann Elert Bode's Uranographia (1801) and in the Bode collection
on chain (830 stars in 109 groups). Give its status plainly at the top: kept by the IAU with its
1930 boundaries, or abolished, and where its stars went.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the crown of Ariadne, given by Dionysus at their wedding and set in the sky (Eratosthenes 5, Hyginus 2.5, Ovid Met. 8 and Fasti 3), made by Hephaestus, or Theseus's crown from Amphitrite; Alphecca/Gemma; T Coronae Borealis the recurrent nova (1866, 1946, the 2024-25 watch); the Blaze Star; Corona as the Camp of the Boors in Bedouin sky (the broken bowl); the Cheyenne and Shawnee crowns; Schiller's Crown of Thorns; the 'northern crown' of the Welsh Caer Arianrhod.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention; the first appearance of each story, who spread it,
and what evidence exists. Where a thread above is marked '(verify)' or '(no: ...)', treat it as a
lead to check, not a fact, and report what you found. Absence of evidence is a finding: say what
could not be traced. For an abolished figure, everything that survives about it, however little.
